Non-communicable disease burden across one diagnostic network in West Bengal

Every structured laboratory result, ultrasound report and echocardiogram recorded by Anubhav Life Care over four years, linked into people and read against standard clinical definitions. Prevalence is reported among the people actually tested, with 95% confidence intervals and age-sex standardisation so that growth in patient volume does not masquerade as change in disease.

Methods and caveats

How the numbers were made

Data

Source: the AKTIV laboratory information system of Anubhav Life Care (Kolkata region, West Bengal), read in full for bills dated 29 October 2022 to 29 October 2026. Structured analyte results came from the chemistry, haematology, hormone, urine, serology, immunology and mixed-report tables (authorised reports only). Hepatic steatosis, renal parenchymal change, calculi, prostate, ovarian and other findings were extracted by rule from the text of every abdominal and pelvic ultrasound report; LVEF, hypertrophy, diastolic grade, wall-motion, valve and pulmonary-pressure findings from every adult echocardiogram; ventilatory defects from spirometry. No narrative text leaves the clinic computer; only flags do.

Linking visits into people

The laboratory has no stable patient identifier (registrations are created per visit), so bills were linked by the same rules as the Sama follow-up pipeline: a valid 10-digit mobile number first; within a number, a fuzzy name match (Jaro-Winkler ≥ 0.88 or same first name) with agreeing sex and birth year (±3); numbers shared by more than 20 names (agents, camps) never link; without a usable number, name + sex + birth year attach to a unique phone-linked person or link among themselves. Definitions

DiabetesHbA1c ≥ 6.5% ever, or glucose in the diabetic range (fasting ≥ 126, post-prandial ≥ 200, random ≥ 200 mg/dL) on two different days or two tests on one day (ADA). A single diabetic-range glucose is "unconfirmed" and is included in "incl. unconfirmed" only.
PrediabetesNot diabetic and the latest test day shows HbA1c 5.7–6.4%, fasting 100–125 or post-prandial 140–199 mg/dL.
DyslipidaemiaLatest profile: LDL ≥ 130, total cholesterol ≥ 200, triglycerides ≥ 150 mg/dL, or HDL < 40 (men) / < 50 (women) (NCEP ATP III). Atherogenic dyslipidaemia = high TG with low HDL.
Chronic kidney diseaseeGFR by CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free) from serum creatinine, age at the visit and sex; "ever" = any value < 60; "persistent" = two values < 60 at least 90 days apart; G-stages per KDIGO. Albuminuria = urine albumin-creatinine ratio ≥ 30 mg/g.
ThyroidLatest TSH: overt hypothyroidism > 10 µIU/mL (or 4.5–10 with low free T4), subclinical 4.5–10, hyperthyroidism < 0.45 (overt < 0.1).
LiverTransaminitis = latest ALT > 40 or AST > 35 U/L (laboratory upper limits). FIB-4 = age × AST / (platelets × √ALT) from same-day results, age ≥ 35; ≥ 2.67 high, ≥ 1.3 (≥ 2.0 at 65+) indeterminate. Fatty liver = steatosis reported on any abdominal ultrasound. MASLD = fatty liver plus diabetes/prediabetes, high TG or low HDL.
AnaemiaWHO: latest haemoglobin < 13 g/dL (men) or < 12 (women), age ≥ 15; moderate-severe < 11. Pregnancy could not be excluded, which lowers the threshold for some women and inflates mild anaemia slightly.
Heart (echo)Lowest LVEF quoted; LV hypertrophy; diastolic dysfunction grade; regional wall-motion abnormality or hypokinesia (ischaemic heart disease); moderate-severe valve disease; pulmonary hypertension (stated, or PASP ≥ 36 mmHg); atrial fibrillation. Structural heart disease = EF < 50%, IHD, valve, PH, cardiomyopathy or AF.
HyperuricaemiaLatest uric acid > 7.0 mg/dL (men) / > 6.0 (women).
StandardisationDirect standardisation to the age (5 bands) × sex structure of all tested adults. Confidence intervals are Wilson. Associations: logistic regression of B on A with age, age² and sex, among adults tested for both.

What is not here

Blood pressure, weight and BMI are not captured by the laboratory system, so hypertension and obesity, two of the largest NCDs, are invisible except through their consequences (LV hypertrophy, albuminuria, fatty liver). Cancer appears only as tumour-marker and histopathology orders and is not analysed. Echocardiography, urine ACR and spirometry are ordered selectively, so their prevalences describe a referred population. Results are per person using the latest value unless stated; "ever" measures can include transient (acute) abnormalities.
