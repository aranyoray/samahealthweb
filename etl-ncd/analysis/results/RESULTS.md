Aggregates only. Generated by `etl-ncd/analysis/` from the AKTIV database; see the HTML report for charts and the methods section for definitions.

## Population

- 174,520 visits linked into 115,829 people (102,882 adults, 12,947 children); 435,472 numeric results, 81,776 ultrasound reports, 8,883 echocardiograms.
- Adults: median age 40, 63.8% women; 27,448 seen more than once, 9,371 followed ≥ 1 year.
- 60,734 adults tested in ≥ 1 of 7 domains; 36,579 (60.2%) have ≥ 1 chronic condition.
